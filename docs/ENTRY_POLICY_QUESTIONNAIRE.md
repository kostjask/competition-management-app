An **entry** means one performance with its title, dancers, category, age group,
format and duration. **Submission** means the studio confirms an entry is ready
for organizer review. It is separate from the studio's application to the festival.
A **confirmation** records what was submitted and when; it does not automatically
mean organizer acceptance unless that is the agreed rule.
