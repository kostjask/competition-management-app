# F11 — Discover the studio and find joining information

Locally implemented by the isolated public HTML consumer; WEB-STUDIO external
acceptance remains open. [Contract](../api/public-pages.md) and
[evidence](../PUBLIC_SITES_VALIDATION.md) separate local checks from release.
[Scope](../PRODUCT_SCOPE.md) · [S18](../ui/public.md#s18--public-studio-website)
· [Shared acceptance](../USER_FLOWS.md#evidence-matrix-for-each-delivered-flow)

- **Actor / prerequisites:** public prospective student or parent; no account.
- **Entry:** studio homepage or direct published teacher/class/timetable link.
- **Happy path:** choose ET/EN/RU → learn about Neposedõ/teachers/classes/ages →
  inspect timetable/location/joining information → use published contact/social
  link, or follow the clearly labelled festival portal link.
- **Data / permissions:** explicit published studio content/assets only. Teacher
  presentation is content, not an account or staff role. No student registry,
  enquiry storage, booking or social-feed synchronization is required.
- **Failure / recovery:** missing/unpublished page has localized navigation;
  content/read failure offers retry where appropriate; unavailable translations
  show the documented actual-language fallback. No account redirect for public
  content and no private draft from preview/cache/assets.
- **Acceptance:** readable text beyond poster images, meaningful alternatives,
  working contact/festival links, reviewed media rights, language/metadata and
  keyboard/assistive/phone/tablet/desktop checks. The director reviews new content;
  social accounts remain available independently. WEB-STUDIO release also requires
  its own domain/HTTPS, deploy/restore/rollback and human review evidence.
